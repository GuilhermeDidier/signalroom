# Shipping and Delivery

## Where we ship

Northwind Cycles ships complete e-bikes to street addresses in the **48 contiguous US states**. Because our batteries are classed as lithium-ion dangerous goods, we can't ship bikes or standalone batteries to Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, PO boxes or APO/FPO addresses.

Accessories without a battery (helmets, locks, racks, lights with removable coin cells, apparel) ship to all 50 states and to Canada. Canadian orders are shipped duties-unpaid; your local carrier collects import fees on delivery.

## Carriers

We use two carriers depending on what's in your order:

- **TrailFreight** delivers complete bikes and standalone batteries. It's a scheduled curbside freight service: the driver brings the box to your curb, driveway or building lobby, not inside your home.
- **ParcelLine** handles accessory-only orders and small parts. Packages are left at the door unless you request a signature at checkout ($3.50).

If an order contains both a bike and accessories, everything travels together with TrailFreight.

## Delivery times

Orders placed before 1 p.m. Central on a business day leave our warehouse the same day. After that, transit times are:

- **Complete bikes (TrailFreight):** 5-8 business days. TrailFreight texts you 48 hours before delivery to set a four-hour appointment window.
- **Accessories (ParcelLine Ground):** 2-5 business days.
- **Accessories (ParcelLine Express):** 1-2 business days, $19.95.
- **Canada (accessories only):** 6-10 business days.

Pre-order models show an estimated ship week on the product page. You won't be charged for a pre-order until it leaves the warehouse.

## Missed or rescheduled deliveries

You can reschedule a TrailFreight appointment free of charge up to 24 hours before the window. If nobody is available when the driver arrives and the delivery can't be completed, a redelivery fee of **$45** applies. After two failed attempts the bike returns to our warehouse and the order is cancelled, with the original freight cost deducted from the refund.

## Inspecting your delivery

Before signing for a bike, look at the outside of the box with the driver present. If the box has holes, crushed corners or a broken strap, write "damaged" on the delivery receipt and take photos. You can still accept the delivery; noting the damage protects your claim.

## Damaged-in-transit claims

If something arrives damaged, you must report it within **48 hours of delivery**. After that window, the carrier won't accept the claim and it can't be processed as shipping damage.

1. Keep all packaging, including the foam and the cardboard spacers, until the claim is closed.
2. Take photos of the box (all six sides), the damaged part, and the shipping label.
3. Email the photos with your order number to shipping@northwindcycles.example.com, or use **Report a delivery problem** in your account.
4. We'll reply within 1 business day and either ship replacement parts at no cost or arrange a pickup.

For a lost package, ParcelLine needs 5 business days after the expected date before it can open a trace, so please wait that long before reporting it missing.

## What arrives in the box

Bikes ship about **90% assembled**. The motor, battery mount, wiring, brakes and gears are installed and adjusted at our workshop. You'll need to:

- Attach the handlebar to the stem (4 bolts, torque 5 Nm).
- Install the front wheel and tighten the thru-axle.
- Screw in the pedals (the left pedal is reverse-threaded).
- Fit the front fender and headlight on Larkspur and Haul 2.
- Charge the battery fully before the first ride. It ships at roughly 40% charge for transport safety.

A multi-tool, torque key and pedal wrench are included. Assembly usually takes 20-30 minutes. A step-by-step video is linked from the QR code inside the box lid.

## Prefer not to assemble?

Choose **Ready-to-Ride Pickup** at checkout to collect a fully built and test-ridden bike from one of our three showrooms (Minneapolis, Denver or Austin). It's free, and the bike is usually ready 3 business days after ordering. We hold bikes for 14 days; after that the order may be cancelled.
