# Northwind Cycles Limited Warranty

## Overview

Every complete e-bike sold by Northwind Cycles comes with a limited warranty that covers defects in materials and workmanship. The warranty applies to the original purchaser only and starts on the date of delivery (or the date of in-store pickup), not the date of order. It is not transferable if the bike is sold or given away.

This page applies to the Larkspur (commuter), Ridgeline (trail) and Haul 2 (cargo) models. Accessories bought separately carry the manufacturer's own warranty.

## Coverage periods

| Component | Coverage period | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Frame and rigid fork | 10 years | Cracks, broken welds, dropout failure |
| Motor (NW-M250 hub, NW-M85 mid-drive) | 2 years | Includes the controller and wiring harness |
| Battery (NW-BAT-500, NW-BAT-720) | 2 years or 800 full charge cycles, whichever comes first | Covered if capacity drops below 70% |
| Display (NW-D3) and charger (NW-CHG-4A) | 2 years | |
| Suspension fork, brakes, drivetrain, wheels | 1 year | Defects only, not wear |

### Battery capacity guarantee

A battery is considered defective under warranty when its measured capacity falls below 70% of the rated capacity within the coverage period. Capacity is measured by our service team with a full discharge test; the percentage shown on the display is not accepted as proof on its own. Normal capacity loss above 70% is expected aging, not a defect.

## What is not covered

The warranty does not cover:

- Normal wear items: tires, tubes, brake pads, chains, cassettes, chainrings, grips and saddles.
- Damage from crashes, impacts, falls, or improper transport (for example carrying the bike on a rear rack not rated for e-bikes).
- Labor for routine adjustments such as brake bleeding or derailleur indexing after the first free tune-up.
- Cosmetic issues such as paint chips or scratches reported more than 30 days after delivery.

## What voids the warranty

Some actions void the warranty entirely for the affected component, and in most cases for the whole electrical system:

1. Using a non-Northwind battery or charger. Third-party chargers can overcharge the cells and are the most common cause of denied battery claims.
2. Installing a speed-limiter bypass, "tuning dongle" or modified firmware. The controller logs any unlock attempt.
3. Opening the battery case or motor housing. Seals carry tamper indicators.
4. Submerging the bike. All models are rated IPX5: rain and splashes are fine, but riding through water deeper than the bottom bracket or pressure-washing the motor is not.
5. Commercial use such as rental fleets or delivery services, unless the bike was bought through our fleet program with a commercial warranty addendum.
6. Exceeding the maximum system weight: 300 lb (136 kg) for Larkspur and Ridgeline, 400 lb (181 kg) for Haul 2.

## Finding your serial number

You will need the frame serial number for any claim.

- **Frame serial:** stamped on the underside of the bottom bracket shell, where the cranks meet the frame. Turn the bike upside down or look from below. It starts with **NWC-** followed by 9 digits (for example, NWC-224018837).
- **Battery serial:** printed on the white label on the bottom of the battery pack, visible only after removing the battery. It starts with **B500-** or **B720-**.
- **Motor serial:** on hub motors it is engraved on the axle flat on the non-drive side; on mid-drive motors it is on a sticker under the motor skid plate.

The frame serial is also printed on your order confirmation email under "Registered Bike".

## How to file a warranty claim

1. Go to northwindcycles.example.com/warranty and sign in with the email used for the purchase.
2. Select the bike and enter the frame serial number (NWC-).
3. Describe the problem and upload at least two photos, plus a short video if the issue is noise, a motor cut-out, or an error on the display.
4. For battery claims, add the battery serial (B500- or B720-) and the approximate number of charges.
5. Submit. You'll receive a claim number starting with **WC-** by email.

Our warranty team responds within 3 business days. If we need to inspect the part, we'll email a prepaid return label for components under 50 lb, or book you into a partner workshop for frame and motor inspections. Approved claims are repaired or replaced, at our choice, and we pay shipping both ways for approved claims within the contiguous US.

Replacement parts carry the remainder of the original coverage period or 90 days, whichever is longer.
