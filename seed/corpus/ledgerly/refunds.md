# Refund Policy

## The short version

We want you to keep using Ledgerly because it's useful, not because you're stuck with a charge. That said, we're a subscription service, so refunds are limited to the situations described here. Anything not listed is decided case by case by our billing team.

## When you're eligible

### Your first payment

If you're new to paid Ledgerly, you can get a **full refund of your first payment within 14 days** of that charge. This applies to both monthly and annual plans. It's a one-time guarantee per company: workspaces created by the same business or with the same payment card don't get a second first-payment refund.

### Annual renewals

Annual subscriptions renew automatically. We send a reminder email **30 days before** the renewal. If the renewal went through and you didn't mean to keep the plan, you can request a full refund **within 30 days of the renewal date**, as long as the workspace has sent **fewer than 10 invoices** since the renewal. After 30 days, or once 10 or more invoices have been sent, the renewal is non-refundable and the plan stays active until the end of the paid year.

### Monthly renewals

Monthly renewals are **not refundable**. Cancel before your next billing date to avoid the next charge. Cancelling stops future charges; it doesn't refund the current month, and you keep access until the period ends.

### Duplicate or mistaken charges

If you were charged twice for the same period, or charged after a cancellation that was confirmed by email, we refund the extra charge in full, with no time limit.

## What we don't refund

- **SMS reminder credits** that have already been used. Unused credit packs bought in the last 30 days can be refunded.
- **Payment processing fees** charged by your payment gateway when your clients pay your invoices. Those fees belong to the gateway, not Ledgerly.
- **Workspaces closed for a breach of the Terms of Service**, such as sending fraudulent invoices.
- **Partial months or partial years** after the windows above have passed.
- **Enterprise subscriptions.** Refunds for Enterprise are governed by your signed order form, not this page.

Service outages are handled through service credits under our uptime commitment for Scale and Enterprise plans, not through cash refunds.

## How to request a refund

Only the workspace **Owner** can request a refund.

1. Go to **Settings > Billing > History**.
2. Find the charge and click **Request refund**. If the button is greyed out, the charge is outside the refund windows above.
3. Choose a reason and add a note if you want.
4. Submit. You'll get a confirmation email with a request ID starting with **RF-**.

If you can't sign in, or the workspace has already been closed, email billing@ledgerly.example.com from the Owner's email address with the receipt number (it starts with **LR-**).

## What happens after you ask

- We review requests within **2 business days**.
- Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method. We can't refund to a different card or account.
- Card refunds usually show on your statement **5-10 business days** after approval, depending on your bank.
- Bank transfer (ACH or SEPA) refunds take **7-14 business days** and must go back to the account that paid.
- Taxes charged on the original payment are refunded together with it.

When a first-payment or annual-renewal refund is approved, the subscription is cancelled at the same time and the workspace moves back to a **free read-only state**. Your data stays available for viewing and export; nothing is deleted because of a refund.

## Currency differences

We refund the same amount in USD that we charged. If your bank converted the original charge from another currency, the amount you receive back may differ slightly because of exchange rates on the day of the refund. Ledgerly can't cover these differences.

## Disputes and chargebacks

Please contact us before opening a dispute with your card issuer. A chargeback freezes the workspace immediately while the dispute is open, and every chargeback carries a $15 dispute fee from our payment processor that we pass on if the dispute is resolved in our favor. Most billing issues are resolved by our team within 2 business days.
